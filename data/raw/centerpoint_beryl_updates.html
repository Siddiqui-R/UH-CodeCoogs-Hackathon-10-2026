Hurricane Beryl Restoration Updates

CenterPoint Energy employees, contractors and mutual assistance crews have restored electricity to impacted customers who are able to receive power. Any remaining outages will be predominantly isolated instances when customers are unable to receive power due to severe home damage or damage to customer-owned equipment such as weatherheads. We are grateful to our customers for their patience as our crews worked around-the-clock and in difficult conditions to get the lights and air conditioning back ​on for our customers impacted by Hurricane Beryl. We also want to thank first responders and local law enforcement for all of their support. ​​​​​
